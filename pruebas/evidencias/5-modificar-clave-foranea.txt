5. MODIFICAR LA CLAVE FORÁNEA  (PATCH /cosechas/:id/granja)
============================================================

Finalidad: pasar la cosecha 4 de la granja 1 a la granja 2 (que existe) cambiando ID_GRANJA.

--- ANTES ---
SQL> SELECT * FROM "GRANJA" ORDER BY "ID"
 ID | NOMBRE          
----+------------------
 1  | Granja del Sol  
 2  | Granja del Monte
(2 filas)

SQL> SELECT c."ID", c."NOMBRE", c."ID_GRANJA", g."NOMBRE" AS "GRANJA" FROM "COSECHA" c INNER JOIN "GRANJA" g ON g."ID" = c."ID_GRANJA" WHERE c."ID" = 4
 ID | NOMBRE              | ID_GRANJA | GRANJA        
----+---------------------+-----------+----------------
 4  | Cosecha de pimiento | 1         | Granja del Sol
(1 fila)
